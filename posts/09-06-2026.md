\- The Bitcoin-gold 90-day correlation hit a six-year high, with both rising after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent indicated the Treasury would repurchase long-dated bonds. ZCash is currently the 9th largest cryptocurrency, and is up 40% over the past week. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-09-04-zcash-tops-1000-etf-inflows-ramp-up-miners-pile-in-413580).

\- Strategy resumed Bitcoin purchases after a 10-week pause, purchasing $370 million BTC. [Link](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/saylor-s-strategy-restarts-bitcoin-buying-spending-370-million).
