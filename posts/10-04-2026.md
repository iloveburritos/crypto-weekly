\- Bitcoin passed $86,700, its highest price YTD, but fell after a weak jobs report led to a small market sell-off in risk assets. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-10-02-bitcoin-nears-highest-level-january-85000-sell-wall-clears-us-jobs-data-disappoints-417570).

\- The top 185 tokenholders of the $TRUMP memecoin will be invited to a November gala dinner with the president. [Link](https://unchainedcrypto.com/trump-memecoin-offers-dinner-seats-to-top-185-holders-as-democrats-eye-probes/).
