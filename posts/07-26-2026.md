**THIS WEEK IN CRYPTO**
\- The Senate's latest version of the Clarity Act includes software developer protections and a revised ethics provision with a sunset date in 2029, clearing what are expected to be the final hurdles before a Senate vote. The bill would prohibit public officials from issuing or sponsoring digital assets, though they would still be permitted to invest in cryptocurrencies. It also establishes a safe harbor for non-custodial software developers, clarifying that they are not considered money transmitters. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/post/409442/senate-releases-latest-clarity-act-software-developer-protections-ethics-provision-sunset-date-2029). [Link](https://www.theblock.co/post/409000/trump-agrees-to-ethics-provision-crypto-bill). [Link](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/07/21/white-house-pushes-senate-democrats-to-take-historic-crypto-clarity-act-ethics-deal). 

\- The London Stock Exchange will introduce all-day trading, five days a week, in 2027 starting with ETFs. [Link](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/stock-market-today-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-07-21-2026/card/round-the-clock-trading-is-coming-to-london-pHT4VoBAcB3E9XG7HaMq). [Link](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-21/london-stock-exchange-to-introduce-nonstop-trading-next-year). 

\- BNY plans to introduce tokenized Treasuries on its private blockchain by the end of 2026, as it aims to support 24/7 settlement and an "always-on" Treasury market. [Link](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/bny-pushes-toward-24-7-treasury-settlement-for-digital-assets). 

\- Tokenized real-world asset (RWA) perps have grown from $85 billion in January to $470 billion in June, with tokenized equities proving more popular than tokenized commodities. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/post/408961/tokenized-equity-perps-drive-rwa-trading-boom-to-470-billion-monthly-volume). 

\- Telegram is rolling out a native non-custodial wallet to more than 1 billion users on the app. The Gram wallet will offer instant, zero-fee crypto transactions. [Link](https://decrypt.co/374001/pavel-durov-telegram-crypto-wallet-billion-users). [Link](https://www.theblock.co/post/409099/telegram-plans-largest-rollout-non-custodial-crypto-wallet-in-history). 

\- Decentralized exchange Hyperliquid plans to introduce prediction markets to the protocol with its upcoming HIP-4 upgrade. Once live, anyone will be able to offer a prediction market on the platform, subject to templates approved by validators. [Link](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/07/20/hyperliquid-plans-to-add-decentralized-prediction-markets-in-upgrade-to-hip-4). 

\- Crypto exchange BitMEx will close permanently in September after an 11-year run. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/post/409495/bitmex-to-shut-down-permanently). 

\- Jack Dorsey announced "Buzz," an open-source, decentralized AI-native workspace for human and agent teams built on the Nostr protocol. [Link](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/jack-dorsey-debuts-chat-app-slack-rival). 

\- Russia passed a landmark crypto bill opening the market to retail investors, though crypto remains banned for domestic payments. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/post/409121/russia-passes-landmark-crypto-bill-allowing-regulated-retail-trading). 

\- Robinhood is considering adding Crypto.com's event contracts to its prediction markets hub. The platform currently supports Kalshi bets. [Link](https://www.wsj.com/finance/currencies/prediction-markets-race-heats-up-as-robinhood-and-crypto-com-hold-talks-9fc211dd) [Link](https://www.theblock.co/post/409665/robinhood-in-talks-to-add-crypto-com-prediction-markets-as-kalshi-rivalry-grows-wsj). 

\- Robinhood CEO Vlad Tenev's X account was compromised, with the hacker pushing a "Vladhood" memecoin scam. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/post/409560/robinhood-ceo-x-account-apparently-hacked-promote-vladhood-memecoin-flagged-scam). 

\- The crypto industry directly employs 34,000 people in the US and is expected to contribute more than $55 billion to US GDP in 2026. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/post/409417/crypto-directly-employs-34000-in-the-us-and-contributes-55-billion-to-the-economy-nca-report). 

\- The SEC agreed to pay $150,000 in legal fees to end Coinbase's 2024 lawsuit seeking internal documents from the regulator on its Biden-era crackdown on the crypto industry. The agreement ends a two-year FOIA legal battle that uncovered lost internal communications at the agency related to its crypto policies. [Link](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/sec-agrees-to-pay-150-000-to-end-coinbase-suit-over-records). [Link](https://www.wsj.com/opinion/coinbase-sues-the-sec-settles-dadfbee5).
