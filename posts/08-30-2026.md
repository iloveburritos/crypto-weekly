**THIS WEEK IN CRYPTO**
\- Bitcoin slipped below $80,000 after Fed Chair Kevin Warsh pledged to bring inflation back to target, reinforcing expectations that rates will stay tighter for longer and cooling some of the week’s risk-on momentum. [Link](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-28/bitcoin-slips-below-80-000-after-kevin-warsh-inflation-vow).

\- Investors are buying both gold and Bitcoin as hedges against renewed anxiety over US borrowing and the dollar, with State Street’s gold ETF seeing $3.4 billion of inflows and BlackRock’s Bitcoin ETF seeing $1.5 billion of inflows this week. [Link](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/bitcoin-and-gold-etfs-draw-7-billion-as-scarcity-trade-surges).

\- The SEC has sent the White House proposed rules concerning the custody of crypto assets for investment advisors and investment companies, which would include eliminating some existing rules. Details on the proposal have not been made public. [Link](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/sec-preps-overhaul-of-crypto-custody-rules-for-investment-firms).

\- Bitwise’s Solana ETF crossed $1 billion in AUM 10 months after launching, making it the first Solana ETF to cross the milestone. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2026-08-28-bitwise-fund-is-first-solana-etf-to-hit-1-billion-aum-413035).

\- USDC issuer Circle is the official front-of-shirt sponsor for Chelsea FC. Jerseys will prominently say “USDC”. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-08-28-circle-strikes-chelsea-front-of-shirt-sponsorship-deal-featuring-usdc-413002).

\- The Dallas Fed reports that tokenized deposits could drain $700 billion from bank lending, as it would allow customers to move funds more quickly in search of higher yields. [Link](https://decrypt.co/376609/tokenized-deposits-bank-lending-dallas-fed).

\- Crypto analytics firm Chainalysis reports that global onchain taxable activity reached $457 billion in 2025, with the US accounting or $113 billion. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-08-27-chainalysis-says-global-crypto-taxable-activity-topped-457-billion-in-2025-412919). \- Charles Schwab is adding Solana, Avalanche, and Chainlink to its Schwab Crypto Platform. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-08-27-charles-schwab-add-solana-avalanche-chainlink-to-crypto-trading-platform-412923).
