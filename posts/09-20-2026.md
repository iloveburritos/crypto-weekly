**THIS WEEK IN CRYPTO**
\- Falling oil prices helped lift risk assets, including crypto, despite the Fed raising rates for the first time since 2023 earlier this week. [Link](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-16/stock-market-today-dow-s-p-live-updates).

\- The CLARITY Act failed to advance in the US Senate, causing Bitcoin to drop as much as 5%. Democrats voted no, saying the bill failed to address conflict of interest concerns related to President Trump’s crypto holdings. [Link](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/cryptocurrencies-extend-slide-with-clarity-act-on-track-to-fail). [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-15-clarity-act-preliminary-vote-falls-short-in-senate-amid-ethics-fight-over-trumps-crypto-wealth-415010). [Link](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/us-senate-blocks-landmark-crypto-bill-in-industry-loss).

\- The SEC will allow tokenized stocks to start trading in the US via Tokenized Securities Venues, or TSVs, as part of its long-anticipated “innovation exemption” for the crypto industry. [Link](https://www.wsj.com/finance/regulation/tokenized-stocks-get-u-s-regulators-green-light-to-come-ashore-c37d956f). [Link](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-17/sec-issues-guidance-to-tokenize-stocks-after-clarity-act-failure). [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-17-sec-releases-innovation-exemption-415324). [Link](https://decrypt.co/378492/sec-innovation-exemption-tokenized-stocks-clarity-act).

\- The CFTC issued a “no action position” for developers of “passive software” including self-custodied wallets, saying it would not recommend enforcement action against them for not registering as brokers if certain conditions are met. [Link](https://unchainedcrypto.com/wallets-can-offer-regulated-perps-without-a-broker-license-cftc-staff-says/). [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-17-regulators-keep-moving-crypto-cftc-follows-sec-developer-friendly-no-action-stance-415425). [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-18-cftc-files-crypto-asset-rulemaking-with-white-house-pressing-ahead-without-congress-415510). [Notice](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9300-26).

\- S&P Global is acquiring open-source smart contract security firm OpenZeppelin to develop security assets, benchmarks, and data services for onchain financial products. Terms of the deal were not disclosed. [Link](https://decrypt.co/378505/sp-global-acquire-crypto-openzeppelin). [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2026-09-17-sp-global-agrees-to-acquire-openzeppelin-in-onchain-security-push-415360).

\- The House Financial Services Committee advanced the American Reserve Modernization Act, which would codify President Trump’s plan to create a strategic Bitcoin reserve. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-16-house-committee-moves-bitcoin-reserve-bill-415317).

\- A House panel advanced crypto tax legislation that would exempt crypto network and transaction fees of $10 or less from taxation and treat mining and staking rewards as ordinary income. The bill also addresses small deviations from the $1 peg for stablecoins, generally treating their redemption value as the tax basis. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-16-house-panel-approves-first-federal-crypto-tax-framework-one-day-after-senates-clarity-act-stumbles-415293). [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-15-house-committee-crypto-tax-bill-414824).

\- Coinbase files to list perpetual futures, known as “perps”, for roughly 50-60 large-cap US stocks such as Apple, Microsoft, Tesla, and Nvidia. [Link](https://www.wsj.com/livecoverage/stock-market-today-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-09-18-2026/card/coinbase-files-to-list-u-s-single-stock-perpetual-futures-f2q6W4mtxLA3hkVHvqcT).

\- Deutsche Bank is launching a crypto custody service for institutional clients. [Link](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-16/deutsche-bank-launches-crypto-custody-service-for-institutions).

\- The DOJ charged two former Robinhood engineers with commodities and wire fraud, alleging they traded on confidential information about upcoming Robinhood Crypto listings to profit over $50k. [Link](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-15-doj-charges-robinhood-engineers-front-running-crypto-listings-hyperliquid-414865). [Link](https://unchainedcrypto.com/doj-says-two-robinhood-engineers-used-perpetual-futures-to-trade-ahead-of-its-crypto-listings/).

\- A group claiming responsibility for obtaining data on 680 Revolut customers with large crypto balances by impersonating law enforcement has demanded 6,000 XMR, worth about $3 million. [Link](https://unchainedcrypto.com/group-claiming-revolut-breach-demands-6000-monero-says-it-hunted-big-crypto-holders/).

\- Robinhood is working to add share redemptions and voting rights to its Robinhood Stock Tokens. [Link](https://unchainedcrypto.com/robinhood-plans-in-kind-redemptions-and-voting-rights-for-stock-tokens-after-amc-backlash/).

\- State-backed hackers now account for roughly half of malicious code written to public blockchains according to Chainalysis. [Link](https://unchainedcrypto.com/chainalysis-says-state-hackers-now-write-half-of-the-malware-hidden-on-blockchains/).

\- Ethereum and Coinbase’s Layer-2 Base have given up on building one shared account-abstraction standard for both networks and will ship competing designs instead. [Link](https://unchainedcrypto.com/ethereum-and-base-abandon-a-shared-wallet-standard-as-account-abstraction-talks-break-down/).
